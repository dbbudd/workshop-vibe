Round 2 · Getting started

Claude

Claude is Anthropic’s AI assistant. In the Claude app, describe your tool in a chat, and Claude builds it beside the conversation as an artifact: a small app you can use straight away, change by asking, and share with a link. In the Shortcuts and Claude session, it is the Round 2 tool.

Best for

The same barrier as Round 1, as a screen students use. Same prompt spine, more ambition.

Start here

The Claude app for Mac, on macOS 11 or later.
Every plan, Free included, can build artifacts.

Hong Kong is not on Anthropic’s list of supported regions for Claude, and Anthropic’s terms allow Claude to be used only from a supported region. A new account also needs a phone number from one. Check with HKIS leadership and IT before you plan a session around it.

Exercise 1: Get the app and sign in

  1. Go to claude.com/download, choose Download for macOS, and open Claude from Applications.
  2. Sign in: choose Continue with Google or Continue with email.

On a Free, Pro or Max account, Anthropic can use your chats to train its models. To stop this, open Settings, then Privacy, and turn off Help improve our AI models. Team, Enterprise and education plans don’t train on chats by default.

Exercise 2: Build from your prompt spine

Fill in the prompt spine from the session with your own barrier, and send it in a new chat.

  1. Start a new chat. If the app offers Chat and Cowork, choose Chat.
  2. Paste your prompt spine, and send it.
  3. Claude builds your tool beside the chat. Use it there, as a student would. It is also saved under Artifacts, in the sidebar.
The prompt spine, filled in

Build a single-screen tool for a Grade 7 student who loses their place in a multi-step maths problem. Show the current step first, then let them tick it off. Keep the language at Grade 5. No sign-in.

To change it, ask in the same chat, one change at a time. If something breaks, choose Try fixing with Claude.

No artifact? Artifacts need Cloud code execution and file creation, in Settings, then Capabilities. It is on unless someone turned it off.

Exercise 3: Test it, then share it

  1. Test it as in Test 01: hand it to a colleague, say nothing, and watch where they hesitate. Each hesitation is your next prompt.
  2. When it works, choose Share on the artifact, then Anyone with the link, and copy the link.
  3. Open the link in a private browser window, to see what students will see.

If the link asks you to sign in, students can’t use it: Claude accounts are for people aged 18 and over. Instead, choose Copy code, save it as index.html, and publish it with GitHub Pages. TBC

➜ Publish it with GitHub PagesThe App publishing guide, step by step: upload your index.html and turn on Pages →

To stop sharing, change Anyone with the link back to Only you. Older artifacts show Publish and Unpublish instead, and unpublishing is permanent. Keep students’ names and personal information out of your prompts.
